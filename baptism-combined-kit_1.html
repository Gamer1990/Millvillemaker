The Bear & the Dove

Introduction

Today we celebrate a big step. When we are baptized, we make promises to Heavenly Father, and He gives us the gift of the Holy Ghost. These three bears and this gift can help us remember what matters.

Bear One: Remember Jesus Christ

The first bear reminds us to remember the Savior and take His name upon us. The Holy Ghost can help us think of Him each day.

Bear Two: Keep His Commandments

The second bear reminds us to follow the commandments. When we listen, the Holy Ghost can guide us toward what is right.

Bear Three: Share the Light

The third bear reminds us to help others. The Holy Ghost can comfort us and give us the courage to lift someone who is struggling.

Closing

Andrew Elam, may you always remember that the Holy Ghost is with you. We love you and are proud of you.
